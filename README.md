## What is Midhkar?

Midhkar is a free, open tool that turns Quran verses into short, professional videos — ready to share on Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, or any platform.

Pick a surah. Pick a reciter. Pick the verses you love. Midhkar handles the rest — synchronized recitation, beautiful typography, and a background that fits the mood of the ayah. No editing skills needed. No watermark. No cost. Ever.

## Why It Exists

The Qur'an is meant to be heard, reflected upon, and shared. But turning a verse into something you can post — with proper recitation, clean text, and a visual that does justice to the words — has always required editing software, technical skill, and time most people don't have.

Midhkar removes all of that. It exists so that anyone — a student, a teacher, a parent, a da'ee — can share an ayah in under a minute.

## How It Works

### 1. Choose a Surah

Browse all 114 surahs with search, Meccan/Medinan filters, and quick-select for popular surahs like Al-Fatiha, Yasin, and Ar-Rahman.

### 2. Select Your Verses

Pick individual ayahs, or use presets — first 3, first 5, first 10, or the full surah. Each verse shows the Arabic text and your preferred translation (English, French, or Arabic tafsir).

### 3. Customize the Look

- **Background** — Choose from a library of ambient video backgrounds, or upload your own
- **Text style** — Amiri, Scheherazade, Noto Naskh, or Kufi fonts
- **Colors** — Gold, parchment, white, or any custom color for Arabic and translation
- **Position** — Top, center, or bottom of frame
- **Platform** — 9:16 (Reels/TikTok), 16:9 (YouTube), or 1:1 (Instagram)
- **Overlay** — Linear, radial, or none — to keep text readable over any background

### 4. Generate

One click. Midhkar downloads the recitation audio, renders each frame, and encodes the final video — all in your browser. The recitation is perfectly synchronized with the on-screen text, with a natural pause before each verse begins.

## The Name

**مذكار (Midhkar)** comes from the Arabic root **ذ-ك-ر** (dh-k-r), meaning remembrance, reminder, reminder of good. The Qur'an itself is called **الذكر** (The Reminder / The Remembrance):

<div align="center">
<p dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.1em; line-height:2;">
تَبَارَكَ الَّذِي نَزَّلَ الْفُرْقَانَ عَلَىٰ عَبْدِهِ لِيَكُونَ لِلْعَالَمِينَ نَذِيرًا
</p>

_"Blessed is He who sent down the Criterion upon His Servant that he may be a warner to the worlds."_
— **Surah Al-Furqan 25:1**

</div>

Every video created with Midhkar is a reminder — a digital sadaqah jariyah that continues to benefit whoever watches it, hears it, and is guided by it.

<div align="center">

> إِذَا مَاتَ الْإِنْسَانُ انْقَطَعَ عَنْهُ عَمَلُهُ إِلَّا مِنْ ثَلَاثَةٍ إِلَّا مِنْ صَدَقَةٍ جَارِيَةٍ أَوْ عِلْمٍ يُنْتَفَعُ بِهِ أَوْ وَلَدٍ صَالِحٍ يَدْعُو لَهُ

_"When a person dies, their deeds come to an end except for three: ongoing charity, beneficial knowledge, or a righteous child who prays for them."_
— **Sahih Muslim 1631**
